# Limitations

1. **Association is not causation.** Cropland/forest and fertilizer/yield results are correlations.
2. **National cost grain.** Land and fertilizer sources are national, while production is crop-level.
3. **Forest measure.** National forest change nets gains and losses and does not isolate primary forest clearing.
4. **Historical capacity is not instant supply.** A producer's historical peak does not prove it can ramp immediately.
5. **Production is not trade availability.** The CFI does not model exports, inventories, contracts, logistics or policy restrictions.
6. **Trade is supporting only.** Producer exposure and exporter exposure are not interchangeable.
7. **Trade XLSX size/truncation risk.** Excel exports of the Detailed Trade Matrix can hit the 1,048,576-row sheet limit; use a normalized/bulk or pre-filtered source before publishing trade results.
8. **35% shock is a scenario test, not independent validation.**
9. **CFI thresholds and normalization ceilings are working modeling choices**, supported by sensitivity analysis rather than uniquely estimated constants.
10. **Water was excluded.** National water-stress data did not support the final cost mechanism; basin-level data would be needed for a stronger extension.
