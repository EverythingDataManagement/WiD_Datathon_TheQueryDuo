# Trade analysis note

Trade was explored to translate structural crop fragility into named importer exposure.

The optional script examines, for each fragile crop, which countries import most from
the crop's leading producer and what share of their imports comes from that supplier.

It is intentionally not part of the CFI because:
- the leading producer is not always the leading exporter;
- domestic consumption can dominate production;
- bilateral import dependence answers a different question from global production resilience.

The uploaded Detailed Trade Matrix workbook is about 119 MB, above GitHub's normal
100 MB single-file limit, and Excel-format trade exports can be truncated at the worksheet
row limit. Do not upload that raw workbook to GitHub. Instead, document the FAOSTAT
source and reproduce the analysis from a normalized/bulk or pre-filtered extract.

Trade is best presented as a supporting rice/soy illustration or as a future extension
that adds safe-to-export capacity, not as a core CFI input.
